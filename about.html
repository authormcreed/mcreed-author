About M. C. Reed

Home About Books

A mother, a dreamer, and a lifelong creator, M. C. Reed draws her inspiration from the mountains and lakes of the Rocky Mountains.

She has spent her life mastering countless crafts, following her curiosity into new adventures, and raising a child who shares her bright, quick-moving mind.

Her stories grow from the experiences she has lived and the worlds she has imagined, reminding readers that even ordinary people can hold extraordinary destinies.
